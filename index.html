About

Data, markets and decisions

I'm an Industrial and Systems Engineering student at the University of Florida (minor in Computer Science, 3.84 GPA) who likes the point where data meets a trading decision.

At ExxonMobil Global Trading I work on the crude desk: forecasting Cushing inventory levels, ingesting weekly pipeline flow data from the Genscape API into Snowflake, and visualizing the supply/demand balance so traders can act faster. I also automated export deal entry with a validated, AI-assisted pipeline.

Before that, I led a team building an EMA and Bollinger Band trading strategy and backtested its signals on historical data. Outside of work, I'm the Professional Development Chair for Theta Tau, running resume reviews and interview prep for fellow engineers.
